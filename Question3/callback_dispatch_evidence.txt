callback_dispatch_evidence.txt

Evidence that all three type callbacks AND the custom drift callback are reached
through function pointers - no if/else or switch holds the monitoring logic:
  * the type callback is chosen from the table TYPE_CALLBACKS[device->type],
  * both callbacks are invoked through process_device(device, callback),
  * every callback re-checks device->type and only then touches its own union
    member, so no union member is ever read for the wrong type.

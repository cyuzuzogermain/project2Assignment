------------------------------------------------------------------
 CUSTOM ANALYSIS: Lexical Diversity Score (LDS)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose
  The LDS is the type-token ratio expressed as a percentage. It
  measures how varied the vocabulary is: the more the text reuses
  the same words, the lower the score.  Together with the supporting
  metrics below it separates "many different words" from "the same
  few words repeated".

Formulas and current values
  LDS                  = distinct_words / total_words * 100
                       = 104 / 168 * 100 = 61.90%
  Repetition Intensity = distinct words occurring 2 or more times
                       / distinct_words * 100
                       = 24 / 104 * 100 = 23.08%
  Hapax ratio          = distinct words occurring exactly once
                       / distinct_words * 100
                       = 80 / 104 * 100 = 76.92%
  Average word length  = total letters in all words / total_words
                       = 793 / 168 = 4.72 characters

Interpretation guide
  Heuristic thresholds for short passages: the score falls as texts
  get longer, because long texts repeat more vocabulary.
    at least 75      : very varied vocabulary
    55 to 74.99      : moderately varied
    below 55         : repetitive
  Current text: 61.90% -> moderately varied

  2. the                      8
